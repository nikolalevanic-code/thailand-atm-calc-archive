# AEON Thailand ATM Research — August 2026

## Purpose

Validate whether the site can safely state that AEON Thailand ATMs currently charge a 150 THB foreign-card fee and are available in Big C/Lotus's locations.

## Key findings

1. The AEON Thailand public location page currently presents a directory for **AEON Branch** and **AEON Lounge** locations. Its extracted content did not provide a current foreign-card ATM network or an ATM location list that could support a 2026 traveller guide.

2. A dated 2024 Travel Stack Exchange discussion preserves an English rendering of an AEON notice stating that AEON would cancel ATM services in stages starting 11 February 2024, with Bangkok termination described as completing on 13 March 2024. The original question and answer should not be treated as an official current-status statement, but it is material contradictory evidence against republishing the old 150 THB claim without newer primary confirmation.

3. Current search results contain conflicting claims. Some travel/community pages repeat the historical 150 THB fee, while a map listing contains a January 2026 note that a listed ATM was gone and referenced an ATM-service closure. These are not sufficiently reliable to establish current availability.

4. The official AEON fee page available in English returns only general navigation headings in extracted text and does not present a current foreign-card fee schedule that can substantiate the 150 THB statement.

## Editorial decision

Do not publish a new AEON “150 THB fee and locations” guide. Remove existing claims that AEON ATMs are currently operating, charge 150 THB, or can be found inside Big C/Lotus's. Replace them with a transparent note: historical guides may mention AEON’s lower fee, but the site cannot verify a current foreign-card AEON ATM network and travellers should check the ATM screen and use the calculator’s manual fee override.
