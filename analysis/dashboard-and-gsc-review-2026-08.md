# Thailand ATM Calculator — Integrated Search & Dashboard Review

**Sources:** Google Search Console, last 28 days compared with the previous 28 days; Manus Dashboard screenshot, last 30 days  
**Purpose:** Assess current traffic quality, reconcile the two analytics views, and prioritise actions toward a $500/month revenue objective.

## Executive assessment

> The organic-search trajectory is positive. Google clicks rose from **126 to 208** in the latest 28-day comparison (**+65%**), impressions more than doubled from **29,403 to 61,017** (**+108%**), and average position improved from **7.85 to 6.83**. The site is now gaining broader page-one visibility while its strongest content continues to attract real visitors.

The important caveat is efficiency. Site-wide Google CTR declined from **0.43% to 0.34%** because impressions are growing faster than clicks. This is not necessarily a failure: broader visibility often introduces less-qualified queries. It does, however, mean that the next growth phase should concentrate on **better query-to-page matching, more direct answers, and visitor-to-calculator conversion**—not merely more impressions.

The Manus Dashboard supports that conclusion. It shows 624 visitors, 678 visits, and 804 pageviews in the last 30 days. Google is the largest listed referrer (266 visitors). ChatGPT and Claude together sent 20 visitors, which is a genuine early AI-referral signal but remains about one-thirteenth the size of Google in the visible data. The site is not yet monetisation-ready because there is no affiliate funnel or event tracking; revenue cannot be attributed yet.

The strongest unmet demand remains extremely specific. The data contains many queries around **AEON ATMs, their 150 THB foreign-card fee, and Bangkok/Pattaya/Phuket/Chiang Mai locations**. ### Recommended content response

Publish a single, carefully sourced guide: **“AEON ATMs in Thailand (2026): 150 THB Foreign-Card Fee, Locations, and What to Check.”** It should open with a plain answer, state exactly when the fee was verified, explain that the result can vary, and list only independently confirmed locations. It should be linked prominently from the existing Best ATM and ATM Fees pages, then link to the calculator. This is a stronger first move than many generic articles because the query demand already exists.

### The dashboard’s sharp decline needs validation

The Dashboard displays a 86–89% decline in pageviews, visits, and visitors versus the previous comparison period, but Search Console shows a 65% rise in Google clicks over the comparable 28-day window. These can coexist only if the dashboard’s previous period included a large one-off source, a reporting/filter change, crawler traffic, or analytics configuration difference.

The dashboard also reports a “searchbot” browser category with 22 visitors. Do not treat the decline as a confirmed collapse in human traffic until the daily chart, expanded referrer list, date selector, filters, and any analytics changes are checked. The appropriate operational response is to investigate the discrepancy, not to change the product in reaction to one dashboard percentage.

## 6. AI referral: useful, but do not over-invest yet

The Dashboard’s 15 ChatGPT and 5 Claude visitors are worth monitoring. They indicate that the site’s factual, concise content is beginning to be referenced in AI-assisted research. The site should keep doing the fundamentals that make such citations more likely: clearly dated factual answers, transparent methodology, source-backed claims, and clean internal links.

However, Google sends about **13.3×** as many visible Dashboard visitors as ChatGPT and Claude combined. Treat AI referrals as a valuable secondary channel—not the primary acquisition strategy. Continue to track it monthly, and create a separate landing-page/referrer view before making dedicated “AI optimisation” investments.

## 7. $500/month: where the goal stands

**Financial note:** This is working scenario analysis, not a revenue guarantee. Actual partner payouts, eligibility, conversion, and display-ad RPM can differ materially by country, partner, and visitor intent.

No affiliate programme or conversion measurement is live yet, so the site has no observed monetisation funnel. The current Dashboard volume is 624 visitors per 30 days. Assuming a hypothetical $15 realised commission per approved referral, the following illustrates the traffic requirement:

| Visitor → approved referral rate | Illustrative monthly revenue at 624 visitors | Visitors/month required for $500 at $15 commission |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5% | ~$47 | ~6,667 |
| 1.0% | ~$94 | ~3,333 |
| 2.0% | ~$187 | ~1,667 |

At the current 624-visitor baseline, affiliate income could be modest once a transparent, well-matched programme is active, but $500/month still requires a substantial step up in qualified traffic, conversion rate, payout, or a combination of all three. Display advertising should remain secondary: at $5–$15 RPM, $500/month would typically require about 100,000–33,334 pageviews per month. It is too early to add ads if they risk undermining the calculator’s trust and usability.

The commercial milestone before targeting $500 is simpler: implement clean, disclosed affiliate CTAs and instrumentation, then learn whether calculator users actually click and convert. Without those data points, revenue forecasting remains speculative.
