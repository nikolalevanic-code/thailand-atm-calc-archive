# Thailand ATM Calculator — Three-Month Search Growth & Revenue Analysis

**Data source:** User-provided Google Search Console comparison export  
**Scope:** Web search; “Last 3 months” compared with “Previous 3 months”  
**Revenue objective reviewed:** $500 per month

## Intent

This analysis tests whether the site’s organic-search progress is sustained rather than a one-week spike, identifies the content and audiences responsible for the growth, and estimates what must change for the site to approach a $500/month revenue goal. It separates observed data from scenario assumptions.

## Executive view

> The project has passed the “is Google finding it?” stage. Search demand, indexed reach, and click volume have all expanded sharply across a full three-month comparison. The site is still early as a business: it averages roughly **95 Google organic clicks per month**, not enough for meaningful display-ad income or a $500/month affiliate outcome. But the data now justifies focused investment in the narrow topics already working—especially **best/cheapest Thai ATMs** and **AEON’s lower fee**—rather than broad experimentation.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Google clicks | 286 | 19 | **15.1×** | Sustained acquisition has expanded materially. |
| Google impressions | 79,349 | 8,369 | **9.5×** | Google is surfacing the site across a much broader query set. |
| CTR | 0.36% | 0.23% | **+0.13 percentage points** | Click efficiency improved, but remains the primary constraint. |
| Average position | 7.69 | 7.00 | **–0.69 positions** | Reach expanded slightly faster than rankings; this is normal when a site begins ranking for more competitive or broader terms. |

The comparison does not show the exact calendar dates, only “Last 3 months” and “Previous 3 months.” Therefore, it can establish direction and scale but should not be used to attribute growth to a single change with certainty. The pattern is nevertheless consistent with delayed indexing and distribution of the May content/metadata work.

## The dominant opportunity: AEON and the cheapest-ATM cluster

The strongest query signal is not generic “Thailand ATM” content. It is a specific set of searches about **AEON’s lower foreign-card fee, the 150 THB claim, and locations in Bangkok/Chiang Mai/Pattaya**. The grouped AEON/low-fee cluster produced 2,757 impressions and 8 clicks, compared with 232 impressions and one click in the previous period.

| Query cluster | Current clicks | Previous clicks | Current impressions | CTR | Avg. position | Implication |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| AEON / low-fee / location | 8 | 1 | 2,757 | 0.29% | 6.4 | Highest-priority new content opportunity. |
| Best / cheapest Thai ATM | 15 | 0 | 1,799 | 0.83% | 8.7 | Strengthen existing lead article and link it to a dedicated AEON page. |
| Thai ATM fees | 7 | 1 | 1,087 | 0.64% | 11.7 | Improve the ATM-fee article’s first answer and snippet relevance. |
| Withdrawal limits | 2 | 0 | 544 | 0.37% | 10.5 | Expand only with verified per-bank details. |
| Wise / Revolut | 1 | 0 | 242 | 0.41% | 14.8 | Commercially useful but currently a lower-volume, lower-ranking topic. |

Several queries demonstrate a high-rank/low-click mismatch. For example, `aeon atm thailand foreign card fee 2026` has 175 impressions at position 4.7 with no clicks; `cheapest atm fee bangkok for standard bank` has 190 impressions at position 4.6 with no clicks; and `thailand atm fees 2026 for tourists` has 249 impressions at position 8.5 with no clicks. This suggests that the existing pages are being selected as relevant but their search snippets, first-screen answers, or exact page focus do not fully satisfy the query.

The appropriate response is **not** to create unverified location pages. It is to publish one carefully sourced “AEON ATMs in Thailand” guide that answers the fee question immediately, states the verification date and limitations, and includes only locations that can be confirmed from reliable sources. It should link from the existing best-ATM and ATM-fees articles, and back to the calculator.

## Audience quality: Thailand and mobile lead

Google Search Console location identifies a searcher’s country, not whether that person is a tourist, resident, or trip planner. Even so, the engagement pattern is meaningful: Thailand supplied **49% of all clicks**, while the United States supplied 47% of impressions but only five clicks. This implies that raw impressions can be misleading; the most commercially relevant audience may be people with immediate, on-the-ground questions rather than broad US desktop searchers.

The practical consequence is to design every article for a mobile traveller under time pressure. State the fee/result early, use short answer blocks, keep the calculator link visible, and avoid sending users through a long funnel before they can act.

## Revenue reality: the $500/month target

**This is scenario analysis, not financial advice or a revenue guarantee.** The site currently averages approximately 95 organic Google clicks per month. It may receive additional direct, social, AI-referral, and referral traffic, but that information is not part of this export; it should be added to the next revenue review.

Affiliate revenue is the more plausible first path. Display advertising requires substantially larger pageview volume and risks weakening a trust-sensitive tool. The model below assumes a $15 average realised commission per approved referral and treats the rate from site visitor to approved referral as uncertain.

| Assumed visitor → approved referral rate | Organic visitors needed/month for $500 at $15 commission | Multiple of current 95-click monthly GSC pace |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5% | ~6,800 | 71.6× |
| 1.0% | ~3,400 | 35.8× |
| 2.0% | ~1,700 | 17.9× |

At the current 95 Google-click monthly average, a 0.5%, 1.0%, or 2.0% visitor-to-approved-referral rate would correspond to roughly **$7, $14, or $29 per month** at a $15 commission. These are deliberately simple models; actual payout, approval, geography, card eligibility, and partner terms can change the result materially.

For display ads alone, the hurdle is much higher:

| Assumed display-ad RPM | Pageviews required/month to reach $500 |
|---:|---:|
| $5 RPM | 100,000 |
| $10 RPM | 50,000 |
| $15 RPM | ~33,334 |

The recommendation is therefore to treat ads as a future supplementary stream, not the immediate target. The project needs a measured affiliate funnel first: calculator completion → card comparison → transparent partner CTA view → outbound partner click → approved conversion → realised commission.

## What to deprioritise

Do not optimise for US impressions alone, add generic Thailand travel posts, or introduce display ads now. Do not expect FAQPage markup or `llms.txt` to produce Google visibility: Google removed FAQ rich results in May 2026 and says `llms.txt` has no effect on Google Search ranking or visibility.[1]

The project’s strongest evidence of product-market fit is a narrow one: travellers want to know **which ATM to use, what it will cost, and how to avoid an unfair conversion rate**. The next build should deepen that promise and make it monetisable transparently.
